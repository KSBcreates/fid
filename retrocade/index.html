PRESERVING COIN-OP HISTORY

RetroCade Archive is a non-profit museum dedicated to rescuing, restoring, and playing vintage 1970s–1990s arcade cabinets and pinball machines.

WHAT WE DO

Coin-operated gaming was the birth of digital entertainment. As original hardware ages, thousands of custom circuit boards and art cabinets risk disappearing forever. Our mission centers on three core pillars:

  • Hardware Rescue: Sourcing damaged cabinets from basements and warehouses.
  • Circuit Restoration: Repairing original CRT monitors and logic boards.
  • Public Access: Keeping machines playable on free-play mode for public education.
